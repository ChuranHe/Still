Reading ambient light…

Nightstand uses the lowest screen brightness and a centered clock on black. The slideshow pauses. Normal brightness and photos return when the room brightens or sleep hours end. Tap the clock to wake for 5 minutes. Times follow the Portal’s timezone.

Little details

Weather

Enable weather to detect your location.

Automatic location uses this Portal’s public IP through IPWHOIS.io; it may show a nearby city or VPN location. No GPS permission is needed. Changes save automatically once the location fields are valid. Refresh uses your current settings.

Photo fit

All photo proportions are welcome. Photos squarer than 4:3 keep their proportions and show in either frame direction. 4:3 and 3:2 fill the matching frame. Other photos fill when no more than 25% is cropped; extreme panoramas fit in full. Only screen-size display copies are stored.
